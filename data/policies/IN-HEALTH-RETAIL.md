# Sahara Health Assurance - Retail Health Shield (India)

Product id: IN-HEALTH-RETAIL. Currency: INR. Policy wording for an individual / family floater retail health
insurance policy. Synthetic wording written for a software demonstration; the insurer, the product and every
figure in it are fictional. Clause numbers are the ids the rule engine cites.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Policy" means this contract, the schedule and any endorsement. "Policy year" means the twelve months from the
start date shown in the schedule.

1.2 "Insured person" means each member named in the schedule, identified by a member id.

1.3 "Sum insured" means the maximum amount payable for all claims of an insured person in a policy year, as shown in
the schedule (300,000 to 1,000,000).

1.4 "Hospital" means an institution registered as a hospital with the local authority, with in-patient beds,
qualified nursing staff round the clock, a qualified medical practitioner in charge and a fully equipped operation
theatre where surgery is carried out.

1.5 "Hospitalisation" means admission for a minimum of 24 consecutive hours of in-patient care; "day care treatment"
means a listed procedure that needs less than 24 hours because of technological advance (for example cataract
surgery, chemotherapy, haemodialysis).

1.6 "Medically necessary" means treatment that is required for the diagnosis or treatment of the condition, is
consistent with accepted medical practice and is not primarily for the convenience of the insured or the doctor.

1.7 "Pre-existing disease" (PED) means any condition that was diagnosed, or for which medical advice or treatment
was received, within 48 months before the first policy start date, and any condition declared on the proposal.

1.8 "Room rent" means the daily charge for the bed, boarding and nursing attendance of the room occupied by the
insured person, exclusive of medical charges.

## 2. Coverage

2.1 The insurer pays the medically necessary expenses of hospitalisation and day care treatment of the insured
person during the policy year, subject to the limits, sub-limits, co-payment, waiting periods and exclusions in
this wording.

2.2 Annual limit. All amounts payable for an insured person in a policy year are limited to the sum insured. Amounts
already paid under earlier claims of the same policy year reduce the balance available, and a claim is payable only
up to that balance.

2.3 Covered expenses include room rent, ICU charges, nursing, surgeon, anaesthetist and consultant fees, operation
theatre charges, anaesthesia, blood, oxygen, diagnostic tests, medicines and drugs consumed during the stay, and
implants that are medically necessary for the procedure.

2.4 Expenses are payable only for services dated between the admission date and the discharge date of the
hospitalisation, both inclusive. Pre- and post-hospitalisation expenses are claimed separately and are not part of
the hospital bill.

2.5 Quantities billed for per-day items (room rent, ICU, nursing, doctor visits) are payable only for the number of
days of the stay, counted as the number of nights between admission and discharge (same-day discharge counts as
one day).

## 3. Room rent limit and proportionate deduction

3.1 Room rent is payable up to 1% of the sum insured per day. Any room rent above that limit is not payable and is
deducted from the claim: (room rate charged - daily limit) x number of days.

3.2 Where the insured person occupies a room whose rate is within the limit, room rent is paid in full.

3.3 Proportionate deduction. Where the insured person occupies a room above the eligible limit, the associated
medical expenses (surgeon fees, anaesthetist fees, consultant visits, operation theatre and nursing charges) are
also payable only in the proportion that the eligible room rate bears to the room rate actually charged. The
proportionate deduction does not apply to the cost of medicines, consumables, diagnostic tests and implants.

3.4 The room category eligible under this policy is shown on the schedule; the hospital's bill must state the
room category occupied.

## 4. Intensive care

4.1 ICU / ICCU charges are payable up to 2% of the sum insured per day. Any ICU charge above that limit is not
payable: (ICU rate charged - daily limit) x number of ICU days.

4.2 ICU days and room days are counted together against the length of stay; the same day cannot be billed both as a
room day and as an ICU day.

## 5. Co-payment

5.1 Where the schedule shows a co-payment, the insured person bears 10% of the amount otherwise payable on every
claim (in-patient, day care and post-hospitalisation pharmacy), and the insurer pays the balance. The co-payment is
applied after all other deductions and before the annual limit is tested.

5.2 The co-payment cannot be waived by the hospital or the claims handler; a settlement that does not apply it is
an overpayment.

## 6. Deductible

6.1 No deductible applies under this product (deductible 0 per claim).

## 7. Sub-limits

7.1 Cataract. Expenses for cataract surgery, including phacoemulsification, the intra-ocular lens (IOL) and the day
care package, are limited to 40,000 per eye per claim. Any amount above the sub-limit is not payable.

7.2 Maternity. Expenses for delivery (normal or caesarean section / LSCS) including the obstetrician, labour room or
theatre, anaesthesia, room and medicines are limited to 50,000 per delivery. Any amount above the sub-limit is not
payable.

7.3 Physiotherapy. Physiotherapy sessions (service code PHY001) are payable up to 20 sessions per policy year.
Sessions on distinct dates within that count are a legitimate course of treatment and are not duplicates.

7.4 Sub-limits apply before the co-payment and within the sum insured.

## 8. Waiting periods

8.1 General waiting period. No claim is payable for any treatment, other than treatment of accidental injury, that
starts within 30 days of the first policy start date.

8.2 Specified diseases. Hernia, cataract, joint replacement and benign prostatic hypertrophy have a waiting period of
24 months from the first policy start date unless the policy has been continuously renewed.

8.3 Waiting periods are counted from the start date in the schedule; a claim inside a waiting period is not payable
in full.

## 9. Pre-existing disease

9.1 Any condition declared as pre-existing on the proposal (for example diabetes, hypertension) and its direct
complications are covered only after 730 days (24 months) of continuous cover from the first policy start date.

9.2 A claim whose diagnosis names a declared pre-existing condition, or a direct complication of it, within that
period is not payable.

9.3 Non-disclosure of a pre-existing condition that is later found in the hospital records entitles the insurer to
reject the claim.

## 10. Exclusions

10.1 Treatment that is not medically necessary, or that is primarily diagnostic, investigative or evaluative
without active treatment.

10.2 Experimental or unproven treatment.

10.3 Cosmetic or aesthetic treatment of any description, including scar revision, rhinoplasty and liposuction,
unless required to restore function after an accident covered by this policy.

10.4 Dental treatment or surgery of any kind, including root canal treatment, extraction and orthodontics, unless
necessitated by an accidental injury and requiring hospitalisation.

10.5 Out-patient consultation and treatment (OPD) not resulting in hospitalisation or day care treatment.

10.6 Treatment of obesity, weight control and bariatric surgery.

10.7 Treatment for alcoholism, drug or substance abuse.

10.8 Treatment outside India.

10.9 War, invasion, nuclear contamination and intentional self-injury.

10.10 Spectacles, contact lenses, hearing aids, and any external appliance.

## 11. Non-payable items

11.1 The following items are never payable, even when billed by the hospital: surgical gloves, disposable
syringes, cotton rolls, thermometers, admission kits, toiletries, mineral water, diet charges for attendants,
telephone charges, television charges, documentation charges, registration charges, visitor passes, nebuliser
kits, hot water bags, slippers, masks (non-surgical), tissue paper, sanitiser, bed pans, urine pots, belts and
braces that are not implants, crepe bandages, spirit and disinfectant, and attendant charges.

11.2 Non-payable items are deducted from the bill at their billed amount before any other calculation.

11.3 Payable medical items include cannulae, infusion sets, catheters, sutures, dressing packs, surgical drains and
implants used in the procedure.

## 12. Network and providers

12.1 The insured person may be treated at any registered hospital. Cashless facility is available only at network
hospitals; reimbursement claims are accepted from any hospital that meets the definition in clause 1.4.

12.2 The insurer may benchmark the charges of a hospital against the charges of comparable hospitals and refer a
claim for review when the charges are unusually high; such a referral does not by itself reduce the claim.

## 13. Claims procedure

13.1 Planned hospitalisation must be notified at least 48 hours before admission; emergency admission within 24
hours.

13.2 A reimbursement claim must be submitted within 30 days of discharge with the documents listed in clause 14.

13.3 The claim is paid to the policyholder's bank account named on the claim form.

13.4 Line dates. Every line on the hospital bill must be dated within the admission and discharge dates of the
episode; a charge dated after discharge belongs to a separate post-hospitalisation claim and is not payable on
the in-patient claim.

13.5 Arithmetic. The amount of a bill line must equal the quantity multiplied by the rate; the bill total must
equal the sum of its lines. Any difference is not payable.

13.6 Duplicates. A charge that has already been paid, whether on the same bill, on another bill of the same claim
or under an earlier claim, is not payable again. An invoice that has already been settled cannot be claimed again.

13.7 The settlement is calculated in this order: the bill total, less non-payable items, excluded services,
duplicate lines and amounts above the room, ICU and sub-limits; then the co-payment; then the balance of the sum
insured.

13.8 The insurer may ask for clarification or additional documents before deciding a claim, and will not decide a
claim on assumptions where a required document is missing.

## 14. Required documents

14.1 In-patient claim: claim form, itemised final hospital bill, discharge summary, diagnostic reports,
prescriptions, KYC document and the pre-authorisation form. Day care claim: claim form, itemised bill, discharge
summary, prescriptions and KYC document. Post-hospitalisation pharmacy claim: pharmacy bills and prescriptions.

14.2 The name, member id and policy number on the hospital documents must match the claim; a document that names
another person or policy is referred back to the claimant for clarification.

14.3 Where the itemised bill or the discharge summary is missing the claim is held and the documents are requested;
it is not settled on the summary bill alone.

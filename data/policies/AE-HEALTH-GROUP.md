# Gulf Shield Takaful - Group Health Plan (UAE)

Product id: AE-HEALTH-GROUP. Currency: AED. Table of benefits and policy wording for an employer group medical
plan. Synthetic wording written for a software demonstration; the insurer, the plan and every figure in it are
fictional. Clause numbers are the ids the rule engine cites.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Plan" means this group medical insurance contract between the insurer and the policyholder (the employer),
covering the members listed in the schedule for the plan year.

1.2 "Member" means an employee or dependant enrolled under the plan and identified by a member id.

1.3 "Annual limit" means the maximum amount payable for a member in a plan year, 150,000 AED.

1.4 "Network" means the list of hospitals, clinics and pharmacies contracted by the insurer for the member's plan
tier, as named in the schedule. A schedule with no network list allows treatment at any licensed provider.

1.5 "Encounter" means one visit to a provider; an out-patient encounter is one consultation and the services and
medicines ordered on it. An in-patient encounter runs from admission to discharge.

1.6 "Activity" means one billed service, drug or procedure on an encounter, identified by its code (CPT, dental CDT,
drug code or internal service code), its quantity, its unit price and the patient share.

1.7 "Patient share" means the part of an activity that the member pays: the deductible and the co-insurance.

1.8 "Pre-existing condition" means any condition for which the member received advice or treatment within six
months before enrolment.

## 2. Coverage

2.1 The plan pays the medically necessary cost of in-patient, day care, out-patient and pharmacy treatment of a
member in the plan year, within the network, subject to the limits and exclusions in this wording.

2.2 Annual limit. All amounts paid for a member in a plan year are limited to 150,000 AED; earlier claims of the
same plan year reduce the balance and a claim is payable only up to that balance.

2.3 Covered services include consultations, diagnostic tests, radiology, medicines prescribed by the treating
doctor, surgery, anaesthesia, the hospital room, nursing and intensive care.

2.4 Services are payable only when dated within the encounter: on the visit date for an out-patient encounter, and
between admission and discharge for an in-patient encounter.

2.5 Per-day items (room, nursing, intensive care, daily hospital care) are payable only for the number of nights of
the admission.

## 3. Room

3.1 Room and board is payable for a standard private room up to 1,500 AED per day. Charges above that rate are not
payable: (rate charged - 1,500) x number of days.

3.2 An upgrade to a suite or a higher class of room is at the member's cost.

## 4. Intensive care

4.1 Intensive care is payable up to 5,000 AED per day; charges above that rate are not payable.

## 5. Co-insurance

5.1 Out-patient. The member pays 20% co-insurance on every out-patient activity (consultation, tests, radiology and
medicines dispensed at the clinic) after the deductible in clause 6.1 has been taken.

5.2 Pharmacy. The member pays 30% co-insurance on medicines dispensed by a pharmacy on a prescription.

5.3 In-patient and day care. No co-insurance applies to in-patient and day care encounters within the network.

5.4 The co-insurance is shown on each activity as the patient share and the insurer pays the activity amount less
the patient share.

## 6. Deductible

6.1 A deductible of 50 AED applies to every out-patient encounter and is taken from the consultation charge.

6.2 Order of calculation. For an out-patient encounter the insurer pays (encounter total - 50 AED deductible) x
80%; the member pays the remainder. A settlement that applies the co-insurance but omits the deductible overpays by
50 x 80% = 40 AED.

6.3 No deductible applies to pharmacy, day care or in-patient encounters.

## 7. Sub-limits

7.1 Maternity. Normal delivery, caesarean section and routine obstetric care are limited to 10,000 AED per
pregnancy, with no co-insurance.

7.2 Physiotherapy. Physiotherapy (CPT 97110) is limited to 15 sessions per plan year on referral from the treating
doctor; sessions on distinct dates within the count are a legitimate course of treatment, not duplicates.

7.3 Sub-limits apply within the annual limit.

## 8. Waiting periods

8.1 No waiting period applies to members enrolled at the plan start date.

8.2 Members enrolled after the plan start date are covered from their enrolment date.

## 9. Pre-existing conditions

9.1 Pre-existing conditions declared at enrolment are covered from the plan start date for members of an employer
group.

9.2 Undeclared pre-existing conditions found in the medical records may be excluded for the first six months.

## 10. Exclusions

10.1 Treatment that is not medically necessary, or is experimental.

10.2 Dental treatment, dental surgery and orthodontics, including examinations, extractions and root canal
treatment, except emergency treatment of accidental injury to sound natural teeth.

10.3 Optical services: eye tests for refractive error, spectacles, contact lenses and refractive surgery.

10.4 Cosmetic or plastic surgery and any treatment to change appearance, including blepharoplasty, unless
reconstructive after a covered accident or surgery.

10.5 Treatment outside the network, except emergencies, and treatment outside the UAE except as shown on the
schedule.

10.6 Infertility treatment, contraception and sexual dysfunction.

10.7 Treatment of obesity and weight control.

10.8 Alcohol and substance abuse.

10.9 Self-inflicted injury, war and nuclear risks.

10.10 Vitamins, supplements and over-the-counter items not prescribed by the treating doctor.

## 11. Non-payable items

11.1 The following are never payable, even when billed by a provider: non-prescription items, cosmetics, vitamins
without prescription, medical report fees, telephone charges, extra meals, companion beds, parking, toiletries,
sick leave certificate fees, administrative fees, newspapers and magazines.

11.2 Non-payable items are deducted at their billed amount before any other calculation.

11.3 Medicines prescribed by the treating doctor and dispensed against the prescription are payable, including
prescribed vitamins.

## 12. Network and providers

12.1 Treatment is covered only at providers in the member's network list. A claim from a provider outside the
list is not payable, except for an emergency admission, which is payable until the member can be transferred.

12.2 A schedule with an empty network list allows any licensed provider.

12.3 The insurer benchmarks providers' unit prices for the same code against their peers; an unusually high price
is referred for review and does not by itself reduce the claim.

## 13. Claims procedure

13.1 Network providers submit claims directly within 30 days of the encounter; members submit reimbursement claims
within 60 days.

13.2 Each claim is submitted as encounters with their activities, each activity with its code, quantity, unit
price, amount and patient share.

13.3 The settlement is paid to the provider's or the member's bank account named on the claim.

13.4 Activity dates. Every activity must be dated within the encounter; an activity dated after discharge or on
another day than the visit belongs to another encounter and is not payable on this claim.

13.5 Arithmetic. The amount of an activity must equal its quantity multiplied by its unit price and the claim total
must equal the sum of its activities; any difference is not payable.

13.6 Duplicates. An activity already paid, on the same encounter or an earlier claim, is not payable again; an
encounter resubmitted under a new claim number is rejected as a duplicate. A resubmission with a changed date or
amount but the same member, provider and services is referred for review.

13.7 The settlement is calculated in this order: the claim total, less non-payable items, excluded services,
duplicate activities and amounts above the room and intensive care rates; then the deductible; then the
co-insurance; then the balance of the annual limit.

13.8 The insurer requests clarification or missing documents before deciding a claim and does not decide a claim
on assumptions.

## 14. Required documents

14.1 In-patient claim: claim form, itemised final hospital bill, discharge summary, diagnostic reports,
prescriptions, member identification and the pre-authorisation form. Out-patient claim: itemised bill and
prescriptions. Pharmacy claim: pharmacy bills and prescriptions.

14.2 The member's name, member id and policy number on the provider's documents must match the claim; a document
that names another person or policy is referred back to the provider.

14.3 Where the itemised bill or the discharge summary is missing the claim is held and the document is requested.
